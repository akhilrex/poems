If someday this happened
that my 7 year old
spent 2 whole days
preparing a piece of art
for a local
inconsequential contest
where the first prize
was a big bar of chocolate
and the event got cancelled
because the organisers messed up
I don't know about you
there will be hell to pay
This I promise
But then
I actually love my child
