As I would cross the many
cities I'd never visit
and villages I would never know the names of
I would look at the people
waiting at the railway crossings
for my train to pass
And wonder.
Why does this person even exist
if he has no role to play in my life
will never interact with me
or mine
He will have his whole life
with trials and tribulations
and joy and celebrations
and I will never get to know 
or be a part of any of those
Now I thank those nameless faces
for helping me realise
The world does not revolve around me
