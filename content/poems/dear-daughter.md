Dear daughter
I know you are mad
I "refuse" to understand you
and that makes you sad
I know you think I'm mean
for withholding joys and favours
and the ice cream
And I know it feels unfair
to be asked to do chores, and homework
and to sit still in your chair
I know I repeat all the things
you don't like to hear
There starts papa's lecture again
that's a real fear
I just want you to know
that this papa that you see
wasn't born a papa
He was also an 8 yr old once
As unlikely as it might feel.
