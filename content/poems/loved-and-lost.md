It's better to have loved and lost
than never to have loved at all
they say
But they don't tell you what to do
when the loved one is in despair
and think you are the cause
when they are unwell
and you have no recourse
when they stop loving you
while you still yearn for more
or even just a little
when you feel you have failed
in making them feel loved
and even worse a selfish fraud
to still ask for their love
Loneliness is misery
I acknowledge that
but sometimes I honestly feel
I would have preferred that.
