title: "Proud"
date: 2026-05-22T08:00:00Z
draft: false
description: "So proud"
---
Leave no chance
to tell the people
around you
loved onces and acquaintences
young one and old
especially the old
that you are proud of them
when they accomplish something
no matter 
how trivial
you will be surprised to know
not many people say that to them 
anymore
