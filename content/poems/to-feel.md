I envy those who feel their feelings
whose eyes go red
when they are slighted
lose their sleep
when they feel a loss
almost vibrating
at the slightest good news
who feel despair and ask
"Why me"
who curse the gods
and powers that be
who swing like pendulums
high both sides
For here I am
who just bobs a little
like a buoy in calm seas
Unable to feel.
