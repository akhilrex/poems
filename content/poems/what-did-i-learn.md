What have I learnt
in the last 40 years
Have I even learnt
anything
in all these years
Well
Everyone is right
and everyone is wrong
Noone is right
and noone is wrong
There are no shoulds
not even mays and cans
This world is made of only woulds
Nothing is urgent
nothing is critical
Except the well being of loved ones
loneliness is better than meaningless crowd
but one good friend is a win
the 'exoticness' of vacations
and the decadence of food
the suppleness of bodies
and the jingle of coins
will not fill the holes of heart
It isn't supposed to be fair
and noone owes you a thing
not the parents that made you
and not the kids that you make
You
is all you have
always had and always will
So be kind
to yourself.
